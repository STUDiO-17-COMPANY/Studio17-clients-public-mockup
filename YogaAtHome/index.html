The Studio

Not just a generic fitness offer.
Your sanctuary.

"Yoga at Home" was born out of a simple desire: to create an accessible, human-centered space in Limassol where wellbeing isn't a luxury, but a shared experience.

We step away from the noise of crowded gyms. Here, consistency meets warmth. Whether you are beginning your journey or deepening your practice, our experienced teachers are here to guide you toward physical and emotional balance.

Our Practices

Movement for every body & mind.

A variety of styles under one roof. From deep rest to dynamic flow, find the practice that meets you where you are today.

Vinyasa Flow

Fluid, dynamic sequences linking breath with movement to build heat and clear the mind.

Yin & Restorative

Deep, passive holds that target connective tissues, inviting profound rest and emotional release.

Hatha Foundation

Alignment-focused classes holding poses longer, perfect for building strength and stability.

Mindful Meditation

Guided sessions to help you ground into the present moment, cultivating inner peace and clarity.
